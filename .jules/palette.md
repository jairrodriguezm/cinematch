## 2024-06-25 - Disclosure Widget Accessibility
**Learning:** React toggles for expanding/collapsing text (like "Read More" buttons) often lack ARIA relationships out-of-the-box in typical Tailwind templates. The `aria-controls` attribute is essential for screen readers to understand what the button toggles, and `aria-expanded` clarifies its state. Also, icons inside such buttons must have `aria-hidden="true"` to prevent redundant or confusing screen reader announcements.
**Action:** When creating or modifying a disclosure toggle widget, always pair it with an `id` on the target element and corresponding `aria-controls`, `aria-expanded` attributes on the button, while ensuring decorative icons are hidden with `aria-hidden="true"`.
